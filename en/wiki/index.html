The official documentation is 200+ pages in English. This wiki gives you the practical route — the order you will actually need things in, with Czech pricing, providers and hands-on experience added.

How this wiki is organised

This is not a translation of the official documentation. A literal translation would only be a second, worse copy — so we pick what people here actually need, and add the local context the original is missing: what hosting costs, which providers you can pay from Czechia, how to get the agent to speak Czech, and the pitfalls we hit while running it.
